Slack and Microsoft Teams

Two integrations post a message to a chat channel whenever SIEMLite opens an alert (an alert that gains more events is not posted again), including alerts an AI agent raises with create_alert. Each has its own card on the Integrations page, a Post alerts of choice (Low, Medium, High or Critical and above; High is the default) and a Send a test message button.

  • Slack: create an app with an incoming webhook, choose the channel and paste the address (https://hooks.slack.com/services/...).
  • Microsoft Teams: in the channel, choose Workflows and pick the template Send webhook alerts to a channel (search for "webhook"). Set the team and channel in both of its branches, save, then open the first step, When a Teams webhook request is received, and copy its HTTP POST URL (...logic.azure.com, ...webhook.office.com or ...powerplatform.com) into the card. The message is an Adaptive Card, which the template posts from the request. Building the workflow by hand from a blank one also works, but the simple editor has no expression box, so the card field can't be filled in there; use the template, or finish the workflow in Power Automate with the card set to triggerBody()?['attachments']?[0]?['content']. Teams adds a line under each card saying it came from a workflow template; SIEMLite can't remove it.

The webhook address works like a password, so it is saved sealed (like the email password), never shown again, and left out of error messages. Only each service's own addresses are accepted, over HTTPS. At most 10 messages go out a minute (alerts raised by hand, such as an AI agent's create_alert, have their own budget of 3 a minute so they can't crowd out detected ones); if more alerts open, the next message says how many were held back. Redirects are not followed, text from logs is escaped so it can't make links or channel pings, and what the far end replies isn't repeated back. A failed post shows on the card. The messages name the rule, severity, who or what it was about, and how many events; they link nowhere, since SIEMLite doesn't know the address you reach it by. API: GET/PUT /api/v1/integrations/slack (or teams) with {"webhook", "min_severity"} (leave webhook out to keep the saved one; "" removes it) and POST .../test.
