Reports

The Reports page (admins) makes a security summary of a period: the number of events, high-severity events, threat intel matches and alerts opened; events over time; events by severity; the busiest sources, countries and threat-listed addresses; the alerts opened (the most serious 40, with the rest counted); and the threat feeds in use.

  • Make a report: choose the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, or a date range (up to 366 days). Preview shows it on the page; Download PDF makes the PDF, downloads it and keeps a copy under Past reports (the newest 100 are kept, and are included in backups).
  • Scheduled reports: daily, weekly (a day of the week) or monthly (a day from 1 to 28) at an hour, covering the last 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days, emailed to up to 10 addresses with the PDF attached. It uses the Email integration; without it the report is still made and kept, and the schedule says it wasn't sent. Run now makes one at once without moving the next run. Times are the server's; a schedule that came due while SIEMLite was off runs once when it starts. Making and scheduling reports is recorded in the audit log.

The PDF is drawn by SIEMLite itself (no extra software): A4, standard fonts, no external requests.

API (admin): GET /api/v1/reports/summary?days=7 (or from= and to= in milliseconds), POST /api/v1/reports/runs ({"days": 7} or {"from", "to"}) makes and keeps a PDF, GET /api/v1/reports/runs, GET/DELETE /api/v1/reports/runs/{id} (/pdf to download), GET/POST /api/v1/reports/schedules, PUT/DELETE /api/v1/reports/schedules/{id}, and POST .../schedules/{id}/run.
